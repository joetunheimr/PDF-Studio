PDF-Studio combines a document scanner, PDF editor and signature tool in one app. Everything is processed on the device only – no cloud, no account, no ads.

<b>Scan</b>
<ul>
<li>Capture multiple pages with the camera, flash/torch, tap to focus</li>
<li>Import photos from the gallery</li>
<li>Automatic edge detection, adjust corners and edges with a magnifier, perspective correction</li>
<li>Filters Original, Color+, Grayscale, Black &amp; white with shadow removal; brightness and contrast</li>
</ul>

<b>Edit</b>
<ul>
<li>Place, move and scale text, date and signatures</li>
<li>Save signatures for later</li>
<li>Merge PDFs, scans and photos, reorder pages by dragging</li>
<li>Rotate, duplicate and delete pages – with undo</li>
</ul>

Original PDFs stay sharp: text and vector graphics are not rasterized. Files are saved to Download/PDF-Studio; PDFs can be shared and received via "Open with". The user interface is in German.
